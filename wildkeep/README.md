# Wildkeep

A zoological park game played on a living 2.5D map. It's a single HTML file that runs offline and can be added to the iPhone home screen.

**Play:** https://lipovseknejc2-lab.github.io/944488/wildkeep/

## Adding it to an iPhone
1. Open the link in Safari while you're online.
2. Wait for the **✓ Saved for offline play** message.
3. Tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

After that it opens fullscreen and works with no internet. Your park saves on the phone by itself.

## What's in it
- **194 species**: big African mammals (two elephant species and two giraffes), Asian, American, Australian, European, Arctic and Antarctic animals, plus birds, reptiles, amphibians, sharks, rays, large fish, marine mammals, spiders and insects.
  - Every species has real-based requirements: space per animal, needs, climate range, group size, diet, breeding and IUCN status.
  - Every animal is drawn as its own individual, with differences in size, colour, pattern and rare morphs.
- **Building**:
  - Curved spline paths.
  - Habitats, ponds, beds, hills and other ground shapes, all drawn by placing and pulling points that can be sharp or round, in any colour.
  - Buildings made from blocks, wedges, arches, cylinders, roofs, domes and glass.
- **Habitats**: land, wetland & pool, walk-through aviary, vivarium, polar, and aquarium tanks with settings for water, temperature, lighting and depth. Deep ocean tanks are the most expensive thing in the game.
- **Mixing species**: a habitat holds one species, or several that are compatible. Predators, aggression, climate and salt or fresh water are all checked.
- **Arrivals**: animals are bought as cheaper young or as adults with a delivery time. They go to the clinic for quarantine and tests first. Rare species need accreditation; critically endangered ones also need a permit.
- **Care**:
  - Kitchen and food stock.
  - Keepers with a specialism and education level, plus vets, nutritionists, geneticists, educators, horticulturists and volunteers.
  - A keeper school that teaches breeding and care techniques.
- **Breeding**: live birth, eggs (with an incubator) or spawning. There is a studbook and an inbreeding coefficient, plus a nursery and a breeding centre.
- **Genetics lab**: IVF to preserve bloodlines, and trait crossing between species (an elephant with horns, for example).
- **Rehab and release**: rescued wildlife goes through treatment, conditioning and acclimation, then a soft or hard release. Captive-bred animals can also be reintroduced.
- **Life certificate** for every animal: age, growth, tests, requirements, diet, lineage and breeding line forward.
- **Reviews and awards**:
  - Three magazines review the park: Field & Fauna, Wild Lens and The Naturalist Review.
  - Twelve yearly awards, plus the Ark Prize.
  - Score checkers and five levels of accreditation.
  - Grinding is possible, but crowding, duplicate cheap exhibits, quick trades, ticket gouging and idle fast-forwarding all lower your integrity score.
