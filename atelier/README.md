# Atelier

Work better, together. Notes, projects, groups, chat and focus timers in one calm place. One HTML file that works offline.

**Open:** https://lipovseknejc2-lab.github.io/944488/atelier/

## On iPhone
1. Open the link in Safari while online.
2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

## Inside
- **Today:** week overview, message of the day, live focus, a ledger of your day, feelings and a quick panel (note, feeling, action, voice) that floats and can be tucked away.
- **Focus:** Focus 15, Pomodoro, Study, Deep work and custom timers. Breaks have their own colour.
- **Notes:** checklists, headings, pinning, sharing with groups and projects.
- **Projects:** small or big, personal or shared. Tasks board, ideas with votes, notes, files, drawing boards, inspiration board, table with charts.
- **Chat and groups:** direct and group chats. Bot teammates reply, vote on ideas and pick up tasks.
- **You:** knots usage (every action is metered like tokens), posters, settings and backups.

Everything is stored on the device. Export a backup in You, Settings to move it.
