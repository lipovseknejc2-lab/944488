# Selvedge

Found a fashion house. A game for phones that works fully offline.
Open it once on GitHub Pages, add it to the home screen, and it saves an offline copy of itself.

## What's in it
- **Design everything:** 70 kinds of pieces: clothing, shoes, eyewear, jewellery, leather goods, caps, socks and underwear. Shape, fabric, lining, seams, colour, prints, techniques, embellishments, reshaping sliders, and jewels & gold on anything.
- **Your brand:** logo with automatic light/dark versions, crest and secondary marks, house patterns (monogram canvas, checks, stripes), banners, website, social media, and a brand voice from quiet to all-logo heritage.
- **Specialist houses:** full fashion house, or a shoe, jewellery, eyewear, bag, knitwear, tailoring or streetwear house, with a development lab for your own lasts, soles, gold alloys, acetates, leather grains, clasps and cloths.
- **Making & selling:** factories with quality ceilings, your own atelier, samples and wear tests, stock / made-to-order / numbered editions, stores, stockists.
- **Being seen:** three magazines, ambassadors, your own models in your looks, and runway shows in 11 cities, each with its own stage and taste.
