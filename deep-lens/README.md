# Deep Lens

An offline, side-view underwater photography game for phones. Dive a submarine down a deep ocean trench, photograph wildlife, edit your shots in the darkroom, get published in magazines, grow a following and win Golden Fin Awards.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any modern browser — no internet or install needed. On a phone, open it in Safari or Chrome and choose **Add to Home Screen**.

## Features
- 124 species × 10 colour forms (1,240 to collect), from reef fish to hadal amphipods and legendary deep-sea giants
- Dive from the surface to the Challenger Deep (~11,000 m) through rock shelves, crevices and vents
- 5 submarines with 5 upgradeable systems each; 8 camera bodies, film rolls and strobes
- Camera mode: tap-to-focus, zoom, aperture, shutter speed, flash, focus peaking, exposure meter, AF/tracking
- Darkroom editor: exposure, contrast, saturation, warmth, vignette, sharpening, crop, filters — graded live S–D
- Magazines, the Bubblr social feed, and seasonal Golden Fin Awards

## Controls
Touch: drag to steer, right slider for dive speed, camera button to shoot (tap a fish to focus).
Keyboard: WASD/arrows steer, Q/E dive speed, C camera, Space shutter, [ ] focus.

Progress is saved on your device.
