# Parlour

A quiet games parlour. It's one HTML file that works offline and can be added to the iPhone home screen.

**Play:** https://lipovseknejc2-lab.github.io/944488/parlour/

## Adding it to an iPhone
1. Open the link in Safari while you're online.
2. Wait for **✓ Saved for offline play**.
3. Tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

## Inside
- **23 games, each with its own story and a hand-drawn mark**
  - Reflex: Lantern, Monolith, Night Mail, Breakline, Ouroboros, Chime
  - Puzzle: Curio, Knotwork, Circuit, Nine, Recall, Sweep, Weave, Fifteen, Tiles
  - Cards: Patience, Spider, Twenty-One, Five by Five
  - Board: Eclipse, Cascade, Draughts
  - Word: Five Letters
- **Mastery:** every game has its own rank: Novice → Apprentice → Adept → Artisan → Virtuoso → Master → Grandmaster. Your Parlour standing is the rank you hold in *every* game.
- **Knots:** the parlour's currency, drawn as a white knot.
  - Games pay Knots, tonight's game pays double, and every rank-up brings a gift.
  - Only 250,000 Knots exist. They are held in the Reserve, and games pay less as it empties.
- **Notes:** invest Knots in two notes.
  - **Sage (green)** only ever rises, and holding it gives a bonus on every game's payout.
  - **Gilt (gold)** grows faster and pays dividends. It can dip, but never more than 12% below its peak.
  - **Patron honours** reward investing and holding.
- **The Cabinet:**
  - 24 collectible pieces with invented makers.
  - 7 themes, including a light one.
  - Card backs, table felts, discs, serpents and paddles.
  - Study furnishings.
  - Boosts, and early unlocks for games.
- **The Study:** a hand-drawn room where you display your collection.

Knots and notes are play money inside Parlour only, never real money.
