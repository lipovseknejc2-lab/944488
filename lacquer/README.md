# Lacquer

A music-career game for phones: build an artist, make songs in a real (synthesized) studio, release them, chase the charts.
Open it once on GitHub Pages and add it to the home screen. It saves an offline copy of itself, so after that it runs with no internet.

## What's in it
- **Studio:** 15 genres (including Afro House, Amapiano, Trap and Drill), 13 drum kits, 39 instruments, chord styles, long notes, hi-hat rolls, log drums and 808 slides. Every song is scored on 9 categories.
- **Lacquer FM:** a radio that grows as other artists release songs. Your biggest hits stay on it for good.
- **World tours:** plan the cities, venue and ticket price, design the tour poster by hand, then market it on Loop.
- **Life:** homes, cars, pets, a fashion brand, fitness and diet (your body changes over time), celebrity dating, and businesses (film and ad deals, songwriting for others, rental property).
- **Loop:** posts, weekly trends, livestreams and sponsored posts.
