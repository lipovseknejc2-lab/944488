leeway

Leeway is the sailing word for drift: the gap between the course you set and the track you actually travel. The mark draws exactly that, and the app is built to give you room in that gap: other airports, other routes, cheaper or better.

The mark

Course, track and the buoy you reach

Ring — where you start. Dotted diagonal — the course you set. Bold L — the route you really travel, with a single 10-unit corner radius. Buoy — where you arrive.

Built on a 48-unit grid with 4-unit modules. The ring and the buoy sit on the same diagonal, so the mark mirrors itself along it. The buoy is drawn about 4% larger than the stroke implies so it doesn’t look smaller than the ring (overshoot). The reversed version is about 6% thinner, because light marks on dark grounds look heavier.

Awards

Leeway Stars

A gold compass rose, the star sailors steer by. It is deliberately not the logo, so it reads as an honour from across a room. Destinations and routes earn one to three from traveller reviews. Everyday rating stars stay plain and rounded so they never compete with it.

Loyalty

Knots

Leeway’s points are knots: a rope knot on a buoy-yellow coin. Earned on every trip, posted when you’re home, spent on rewards. Status runs Harbour, Reef, Current, Horizon, Polaris.
