# Saltglass

Run a luxury spirits house on a tropical island. A game for phones that works fully offline.
Open it once on GitHub Pages, add it to the home screen, and it saves an offline copy of itself.

## What's in it
- **Nine spirits:** rum, agave, gin, whisky, arrack, liqueur, vodka, brandy and absinthe. Each has its own machines, and every machine changes the flavour.
- **The distillery:** lay out the floor by touch (tap, drag, nudge, rotate, tidy up). Grow cane, agave, botanicals, grain, vines and palms on your estate, and run tours for visitors.
- **Flavour:** ten flavour notes, casks and finishes, bottling strength, blending, and specialists with signature flavours.
- **Special editions:** add rare flavours (saffron, yuzu, truffle, oud, gold leaf…) and rare casks for unique, numbered bottles.
- **Design everything:** sculpt bottles, glass, textures, closures, foil type and seals, plus your logo, social posts, website, shop signs and magazine adverts.
- **Selling:** a cellar door, an online shop, boutiques in ten cities, bars and clubs, resorts and grand hotels, auctions, and signature cocktails on menus.
- **Aura:** how the world sees you. Perception, taste tribes, a market map against rival brands, Lustre magazine, patrons, seals and the world map.
